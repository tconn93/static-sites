# about the brand

        

          

            

              nbd.sh stands for no big deal — the two-letter
              extension is the shell you live in, and the attitude is the whole brand.

# why it's badass

        
Six rules of the shrug-and-ship. Memorize them. Live them.


        

          

            

              
            

            
Speed is silence

            
The fastest fix is the one nobody had time to worry about. If they saw the outage, you were slow.

            fix && deploy # 41s
          


          

            

              
            

            
Ice in the veins

            
Prod is down, execs are pinging, dashboards are red. Your heart rate: resting. Your reply: "on it."

            panic=false; proceed
          


          

            

              
            

            
One-liners or nothing

            
Why write a service when a pipe will do? Elegance is deleting 4,000 lines and replacing them with 12.

            -4,000 / +12 LOC
          


          

            

              
            

            
Understatement as art

            
"Minor cleanup" — the commit message on a full rewrite of the billing engine. Merged. No comments.

            git commit -m "minor cleanup"
          


          

            

              
            

            
Friday deploys

            
Scared of Fridays? That's a testing problem, not a calendar problem. We ship when it's ready. It's ready.

            cron: 0 17 * * FRI
          


          

            

              
            

            
Receipts, not war stories

            
No embellishment needed. The graphs speak. The uptime speaks. You? You just shrug.

            uptime --brag=off
